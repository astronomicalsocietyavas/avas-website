Who We Are

About Our Society

The Astronomical Society of Asoka Vidyalaya is dedicated to building interest in astronomy and space science among students. We organize observation sessions, astronomy workshops, quiz preparation programs, school events, and educational activities to help young minds understand the beauty and science of the universe.

What We Do

Our Activities

🔭

Observation Nights

Telescope observations, star identification, Moon and planet viewing, and practical night-sky learning.

🌌

Astronomy Workshops

Sessions about stars, planets, celestial coordinates, astrophysics, cosmology, and space exploration.

🏆

Quiz Competitions

Training and participating in astronomy quiz competitions around Sri Lanka with teamwork and dedication.

🚀

Rocketry & Space Science

Learning basic rocketry, space missions, satellites, and the technology behind modern astronomy.
